Help Center & FAQ

Find quick answers and instructions to help you navigate CodeTogether.

🔑 How do I register or sign in?

Click "Register" on the main page, type your email, and click "Send OTP". Enter the received OTP and your chosen password. If testing locally without an email server, the OTP will be displayed in the dev response alert.

🔗 How do I host a code room & share a link?

Open the Compiler section in your dashboard and click the "Host Code Room" button. A unique shareable room URL (e.g. main.html?room=CT-ROOM-...) will be generated and copied to your clipboard. Anyone clicking this link will open your room!

👤 What is Guest Mode?

Guest Mode lets you explore courses and documentation without creating an account. Features like Compiler, Visualizer, Real-Time Chat, and Dashboard tracking require signing in.

💻 How do I compile code with stdin?

Navigate to the Compiler section, choose your programming language (Python, Java, C++, C, JavaScript, Go, etc.), enter any input values in the "Input Values" textarea, and click "Run".

🔒 How do I reset my password if forgotten?

Click "Forgot password?" on the login popup, enter your registered email, and submit. You will receive an OTP and be redirected to the Reset Password page.
